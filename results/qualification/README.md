# Handoff and initialization qualification

These compact receipts support the practical contracts in
[INTEGRATION.md](../../docs/INTEGRATION.md). They preserve the selected source cases,
seeds, measured implementation hashes, eligibility and observed outcomes. Source
container names and hashes identify the qualification inputs. These panels measure
handoff behavior, initialization and numerical agreement on the recorded cases.

`static-handoff.json` compares causal 80%, 90% and 97% target-edge crossings on 192
preselected real-source cases. It retains rejection reasons and both paired and
all-eligible summaries. Landing comparisons use the same valid paired sources.

`assisted-start.json` compares filtered-anchor, observed-anchor and known-zero
initialization on 48 real cases and 11 prescribed synthetic cases, each with four
sampling seeds. The same real targets, native contexts and policy are used across
mappings. The actual-position mapping preserves physical coordinates. Early
continuation and final tracking are measured separately. Recorded target ellipses
can be wide, so inside-target outcomes are not exact point landing.

The public source adapters and selected-cohort recipes reconstruct the underlying
recordings.

In `assisted-start.json`, `source_panel` retains the inherited case selection and
its original intent-only protocol. The top-level `protocol`, `summary` and
`synthetic_edges` describe the final three-mode composed measurements. Their scopes
are intentionally separate.

`continuous-numerics.json` retains the accepted native/ONNX optimization comparisons,
including matched-seed endpoint errors, discrete choices, mode signatures and
production-pruning parity.
